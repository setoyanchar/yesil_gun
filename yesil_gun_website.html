Between fate and shadow

A door. A secret. A turning point.

Set in England in 1868, Yeşil Gün begins with the strange pull of an old mansion and the secrets gathered around it. As the past presses closer, the line between coincidence and destiny begins to blur.

A literary mystery about fate, atmosphere, and the shadows that wait just beyond the familiar.

“Fate has a curious way of finding the doors we leave unopened.”A thought from the world of Yeşil Gün
The setting

England, 1868.

Beyond the quiet lanes sits an imagined estate: Ashcombe Manor. Its overgrown gardens, locked rooms, and long corridors hold the atmosphere of another age. The place is fictional, but its mysteries belong to the reader.
